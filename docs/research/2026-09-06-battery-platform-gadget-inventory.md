## EU/UK platforms

Cross-compatibility matters more than a separate EU catalog: EU voltage/connector standards differ
by brand, so not every US platform has a EU equivalent, and vice versa.

- **Milwaukee M18 / M12** — identical hardware and connector in the EU/UK as in the US (Screwfix
  stocks M18 batteries: screwfix.com/p/milwaukee-m18-b5-18v-5-0ah-li-ion-redlithium-battery/853fj).
  **All Milwaukee M18/M12 gadget rows in this catalog are valid for EU/UK owners of the same
  platform**, sourced from the same Milwaukee/US retail listings above (Milwaukee's fan/radio/
  inverter line is sold under the same M18 branding internationally).
- **Makita 18V LXT** — identical hardware/connector worldwide (Screwfix stocks LXT batteries:
  screwfix.com/p/makita-632f15-1-18v-5-0ah-li-ion-lxt-battery/2721v). **Makita LXT fan rows are
  valid for EU/UK LXT owners.**
- **DeWalt** — the EU/UK line is "DeWalt XR 18V", a different voltage/connector family from the
  US "20V MAX" line. **DeWalt rows in this catalog do NOT apply to EU/UK DeWalt XR owners** — no
  verified XR-specific gadget listings were found this pass; XR is marked not-yet-covered.
- **Ryobi ONE+** — EU/UK ONE+ is also 18V but sold through different retail channels (Robert Bosch/
  TTI Europe rather than Home Depot); no EU-specific gadget listings verified this pass. Not
  cross-compatible with confidence without further verification — marked not-yet-covered for EU.
- **Bosch Professional 18V, Bosch Green 12V/18V, Einhell Power X-Change 18V, AEG Powertools 18V,
  Metabo 18V/CAS, WORX Power Share (EU), Parkside X20V Team (Lidl-exclusive), Ferrex X-Change
  (Aldi-exclusive)** — EU/UK-only platforms with no US equivalent. No verified non-tool gadget
  accessories (fans, speakers, etc.) were found for any of these in this pass — battery/charger
  listings only. All marked not-yet-covered.
