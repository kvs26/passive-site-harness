# load-tested-everyday-bag-patterns

Status: candidate
Origin: ../../runs/2026-10-10-scout.md
Strength: **weak** — a specific buyer-information advantage survived the focused incumbent probe; production, exact paid demand, AIO and economics remain unresolved.

## Idea

Original lined everyday-bag pattern PDFs with design-specific before/after load measurements, strap changes and physically tested yarn options. Each design would pair complete finishing instructions with results under named, repeatable conditions, helping a maker choose yarn and strap length before starting.

The designs, samples, measurements and tested US yarn variants are **proposed work**, not existing assets. Recorded sample behavior would not guarantee identical results for every maker or certify a carrying capacity.

- `crochet bag pattern`: **50,000** average monthly-search bucket; `crochet patterns pdf`: **500**, shared across the whole crochet niche. Source: [user-supplied Keyword Planner export](../../../demand-csv/2026-10-09-paid-downloads-screen4.csv), US, September 2025–August 2026.

- Frequency: repeated reference during a project, with possible reuse for another bag. Repeat-purchase frequency and willingness to pay for comparative test results are unknown.
- Existing accounts of unusable stretch and uncertain substitutions support the practical problem. They do not measure its prevalence or establish a representative US audience.

## Edge

- **Original patterns:** independently designed bags, complete lining/strap instructions, original photographs and diagrams, technical editing and maker testing.
- **Hand-verified test evidence:** actual samples of each design/yarn combination, with gauge, lining configuration, applied load, duration and measurement method recorded alongside before/after body dimensions and strap changes. The protocol and results still need to be produced.
- **Buyer action:** compare tested yarn variants and observed strap behavior before choosing supplies and finished strap length; no calculator or generated dataset is required under the user-approved product scope.
- **Discovery surface:** original design pages with real sample photographs and an accessible test-results preview, leading to a fixed paid PDF. Acquisition performance is unknown.
- **Monetization route:** direct original-product downloads, explicitly authorized by the user. Public storefront/sample pages can remain static and use external checkout/file delivery; no platform has been selected.
- **Production/upkeep:** design, samples, tests, editing and photography are initial work; support, errata and discontinued-yarn substitutions remain ongoing. Owner capability, costs and the three-hours-weekly upkeep target are unknown, making this weak rather than automatically blocked.

## Hook

**Choose yarn and strap length for an original lined everyday bag using that design's measured loaded dimensions and strap changes under published repeatable conditions, alongside its finishing instructions.**

The useful distinction is design-specific decision evidence, not another pattern bundle or a generic claim that a bag is sturdy. The opened everyday-bag offers supply construction guidance, qualitative testing claims or limited anecdotes; none of their inspected public pages supplies this comparison in full.
