# crochet-patterns

## Target market

US hobby crocheters buying original downloadable patterns and pattern collections for bags, blankets, flowers and amigurumi.

## Rationale

Chosen by the user on 2026-10-10. The user approved ideas beyond tools/datasets and direct sales of paid downloads. The product would be original patterns with clear instructions and physical making/testing evidence.

The [user-supplied Keyword Planner export](../demand-csv/2026-10-09-paid-downloads-screen4.csv) covers US searches, September 2025–August 2026. Ten crochet terms contain nine numeric buckets totaling **265,500** and one blank. The terms overlap and include free-resource intent; this is not unique traffic or paid-buyer demand. `crochet patterns pdf` is 500. Full measurements remain in the linked CSV export.
