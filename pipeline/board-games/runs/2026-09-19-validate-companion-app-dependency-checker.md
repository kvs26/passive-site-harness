1. **Demand** — `scripts/fanout.py` (Google Suggest, US) on "mansions of madness app" and "xcom board game app" surfaced recurring "without the app" / "not working" phrasing across 2 independent games; several follow-up autocomplete queries hit connection resets and were not retried a third time. boardgames.stackexchange.com (`scripts/pain-fetch.py stackexchange`) surfaced a direct mandatory/optional confusion thread for Mansions of Madness (q/47031); most other StackExchange/HN/Lemmy queries were noise or empty. Reddit dig via `pain-fetch.py reddit` was polluted by an unrelated, generically-named third-party app "Board Game Companion" (Nezbo, BGG-collection sync tool) — ruled out as a false positive by opening `r/boardgames/comments/1904v1x/board_game_companion_app_replacement/` directly. The dig-turning finds were two independent, differently-platformed threads on the **future-proofing half** of the hook: Facebook "Solo Board Gamers" group ("What happens to games that require apps when support...", 160+ comments) and Reddit r/MansionsOfMadness2E ("Will I always be able to play this game?", top answer: "The app on the phone might get phased out at some point..."). **Demand: strong** — both halves of the hook (mandatory/optional fact; future-proofing fear) are independently evidenced across ≥2 platforms/games each.
2. **SERP, AIO & competitor weakness** — Bing (`board game companion app required optional platform support checker`) reproduced the BGG "Games with Official Companion Apps" geeklist (static, unmaintained) plus surfaced **workswithchecker.com**, a broad AI-generated compatibility-checker network; opened directly and confirmed it has no board-game category today (not an incumbent on this hook, but the same competitor-shape documented killing ideas in other niches). Ran the manual AIO checkpoint (3 head queries, `&udm=14` control, 2026-09-19): `is the mansions of madness companion app required to play` rendered a **full AI Overview** that completely answers the mandatory/optional question plus platform availability plus edition distinction, and proactively offers to find "alternative fan-made apps or conversion kits." `is the xcom board game app required to play offline` and `chronicles of crime app required offline mode` did not render a full AIO in this session (still "Thinking/Generating" at read-time) but top web-result snippets already gave complete direct answers for both. A fourth query, `what happens if mansions of madness app is discontinued`, returned **no synthesized answer** — only scattered, dated, inconclusive forum threads (the same Facebook/Reddit threads from signal 1). **Signal graded weak, not kill-level**: the "is it required" fact is now commodity-answered by AIO/snippets across every game tested (3/3), but the harder differentiator (current support status + fallback) has no AIO answer and remains a real wedge.
3. **Hook depth** — the future-proofing fear is independently evidenced (Facebook + Reddit, both predating this idea, both unanswered by any synthesized current-status source). **Hook depth: strong.**
4. **Monetization** — reused this session's Amazon Associates Operating Agreement fetch (same day, same program, from the sibling token-capsule-shopping-planner dig): program mechanics confirmed (tagged Special Links, 24h→89-day cart-add window, mandatory disclosure); exact Toys & Games rate still login-gated/unverified. Outputs (base game or a recommended non-digital-dependent alternative) are linkable, but most rows have no purchasable "fix" for the core lookup itself. **Monetization: weak** — confirms rather than overturns scout's original read; narrowest route of the batch.
5. **Feasibility & sustainable upkeep** — confirmed real, checkable sources exist (app store listings with update dates, an actively-maintained Fantasy Flight Games news feed, community reports), but no bottom-up hrs/week estimate was produced this run, and per-row "is support still current" verification is inherently recurring rather than one-time. **Feasibility: weak**, matching scout's "highest maintenance-risk of the five" read.
6. **Data moat** — the judgment-layer barrier (synthesizing store dates + publisher statements + community reports per game) is real, but the long-tail surface is thin: bounded to the ~10-15 named digital-hybrid games in idea.md's own scoping. **Data moat: weak.**
