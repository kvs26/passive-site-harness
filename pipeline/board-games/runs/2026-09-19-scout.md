### Gate reads (candidates 1–5; candidate 6 already killed above)

**1. Companion-App Dependency & Future-Proofing Checker**
- Gate 1 (AIO): predict-from-form — this is a multi-input lookup (game/edition × device/platform), not a single-fact query; low AIO risk, unconfirmed.
- Gate 3 (data moat): pass, but **highest maintenance-risk of the five** — app support status changes with publisher decisions/store delistings; needs recurring re-verification (app store listing checks, publisher statements, community reports of servers going dark). Within the ~3hr/week cap only if scoped to a bounded list of digital-hybrid games (Alchemists, XCOM, Chronicles of Crime, Detective, Space Cadets, Mansions of Madness 2e, Sherlock Holmes Consulting Detective digital companion, District Noir, etc.) rather than "all games."
- Gate 4: pass (see probe).
- Gate 7 (monetization): **weakest of the five.** The tool answers a pre-purchase or already-own worry, but doesn't cleanly point at a purchasable product — no expansion/replacement/accessory to link on most rows. Affiliate route is thin (link to the base game itself, or to a physical/non-digital alternative it recommends instead — legitimate but weaker than the others). Passes gate 7 narrowly on that alternative-recommendation angle; flagged weak.
- **Verdict: candidate, marked weak on monetization.**

**2. Convention Carry-Bag / Game-Load Packing Optimizer**
- Gate 1: predict-from-form — multi-input geometry calculator (box dimensions × bag dimensions × orientation), top of the AIO-resistance ranking; low risk.
- Gate 3: pass — box dimensions are stable, publicly listed (publisher sites, BGG "Dimensions & Weight" field, retailer listings) and bag/case dimensions come from manufacturer specs; merge of two verified sources, low ongoing upkeep (new boxes/bags added incrementally).
- Gate 4: pass (see probe) — no computed tool exists, only manual trial-and-error content.
- Gate 7: pass — **affiliate route is clean and dual-sided**: travel/storage bags & cases (Amazon, or specialty sellers like Meeple Mover / Battle Foam-style brands) plus the games themselves; travel/storage is also a reasonable-RPM ad-adjacent category.
- **Verdict: candidate, strong.**

**3. Errata & Printing Correction Kit Builder**
- Gate 1: predict-from-form — per-edition lookup + printable checklist, not a single-fact query; low risk.
- Gate 3: pass, with the strongest **hand-verification moat** of the six — errata is scattered across publisher PDF/FAQ pages, BGG forum threads, and designer clarifications, with no aggregator; matching errata to a *specific printing* (box-lid codes, ISBNs, print-run markers) is exactly the "no AI farm can shortcut this" judgment layer. Upkeep is real (new errata rounds ship periodically) but bounded per popular title.
- Gate 4: pass — confirmed unmet, repeated pain (see probe: 5 independent threads across Reddit/BGG/Facebook, none answered).
- Gate 7: pass — affiliate route to replacement components (publisher direct-order pages, or Amazon for reprinted expansions/2nd-editions where the fix is "buy the corrected version").
- **Verdict: candidate, strong.**

**4. Legacy Campaign Reset / Resale-Readiness Planner**
- Gate 3: pass but narrower than framed — the differentiated slice is "which official recharge/reset product + replay-vs-resale path", since general completeness/valuation is already partly served by AI scanner apps (see probe).
- Gate 4: **near-miss, not a clean pass.** Emitting weak per hard rules (near-miss on gates other than 4 may be emitted weak; this is judged a partial/diluted overlap on one half of the hook, not the full hook, so it clears gate 4 itself on the reset/reissue-routing half, but the resale-completeness half is exposed to fast-moving general AI-scanner-app competition).
- Gate 7: pass — affiliate to the official reset/recharge product or replacement components.
- **Verdict: candidate, marked weak — competitive exposure to general AI collectible-scanner apps on the resale-completeness half.**

**5. Token Capsule Shopping Planner**
- Gate 3/4: pass (see probe) — genuinely no tool exists.
- Gate 7: pass narrowly — affiliate route exists (capsule multi-packs, ~$10–20 order value on Amazon) but this is the **lowest ceiling of the six**: a single accessory category, small basket size, and a niche-within-a-niche (only applies to token-heavy Euro games with round wooden/cardboard tokens). Long-tail surface is also the thinnest (per-game capsule specs, not per-game pages people search for on their own).
- **Verdict: candidate, marked weak — smallest addressable niche and lowest monetization ceiling of the batch.**

### Emitted candidates (5 of 6; 1 killed)

Ranked by combined hook strength + monetization + demand breadth:
