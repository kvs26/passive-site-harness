# Dossier: Companion-App Dependency & Future-Proofing Checker

**Per-edition companion-app mandatory/optional + current-support-status + fallback checker for a bounded list of digital-hybrid board games — real, independently-evidenced *community* demand on both halves of the hook, but Keyword Planner data (ingested 2026-09-20) shows almost none of that demand shows up as measurable Google search volume, the "is the app required" fact is commodity-answered by Google AI Overviews across every game tested, monetization stays the weakest route of the batch, and the long-tail page surface is thin (bounded to a small named game list). Confidence: high.**

**Evidence dated 2026-09-19 — SERP/AIO evidence expires 2026-10-17. Pre-build re-probe mandatory regardless. (Keyword Planner CSV ingested 2026-09-20 as a top-up; SERP/AIO not re-probed this run, so the original expiry stands.)**

## Signal scorecard

**Demand: strong** — the hook has two halves (mandatory/optional fact; current-support-status + future-proofing fear), and both recur independently across different games and platforms.
  - Autocomplete (Google Suggest, US, `scripts/fanout.py`, 2026-09-19): "can you play mansions of madness without the app", "mansions of madness without the app", "mansions of madness app not working", "mansions of madness app no sound", "xcom board game without app" — recurring "is it required / does it work" phrasing across 2 independent games.
  - boardgames.stackexchange.com (2026-09-19): "Do all players interact with the Mansions of Madness app, or does one player act as GM?" — direct confusion about mandatory/optional role — https://boardgames.stackexchange.com/q/47031
  - Reddit (Google-indexed, 2026-09-19): "Mansions of Madness app?" r/boardgames — top answer states plainly "2nd edition does require the app to play. 1st edition does not require it..." — recurring question, not a one-off.
  - **Future-proofing half, independently evidenced on two different platforms:** Facebook "Solo Board Gamers" group thread "What happens to games that require apps when support..." (160+ comments, 5 years old but still surfaced/cited) — "Am I the only one sort of worried about games that require an app to play, such as Mansions of Madness 2ed?"; and Reddit r/MansionsOfMadness2E "Will I always be able to play this game?" (8 comments, 3 years old) — top answer: "The app on the phone might get phased out at some point - another 64bit type thing perhaps..." Both are direct hits on the exact "will this become unplayable" fear the idea's hook targets.
  - **Keyword Planner top-up (ingested 2026-09-20, `scripts/planner-report.py`):** of 19 requested keywords across head/variants/hook-specific/future-proofing/monetization-CPC groups, only **2 registered any measurable volume at all** — "board game companion app" (50 bucket, 0% 3-mo, 0% YoY, Competition Low) and "xcom the board game price" (50 bucket, -100% YoY). The other 17 — including every one of the "Variants" and "Future-proofing" queries the request flagged as the two actual halves of the hook ("is the companion app required to play", "board game app discontinued", "will mansions of madness app always work", etc.) — came back **absent from the export (zero/no measurable data)**. Per the rubric, a ≤50 bucket / absent-from-export result gives **no support toward strong** and Demand falls back to the base (qualitative) rules, so the grade stays **strong** on the community evidence above — but this is a real, dated fact worth flagging: the community recurrence documented here is largely happening in forums/Facebook, not as a distinct Google-search query pattern with detectable volume. This bears directly on the traffic sanity-check below.

**SERP, AIO & competitor weakness: weak** — dated 2026-09-19.
  - **SERP:** Bing for `board game companion app required optional platform support checker` surfaces the BGG geeklist "Games with Official Companion Apps (And Where to Find Them)" (static, unmaintained, incomplete — confirmed by scout) and, new this run, **workswithchecker.com** — a broad AI-generated "device/software/game/accessory compatibility checker" network (categories: Game & Device, Smart Home Hub, EV Charger/Vehicle, Camera/Lens Mount, Printer/Ink, Robot Vacuum, Baby Monitor, etc.). Opened directly: **it does not cover board games or tabletop companion apps at all** — not an incumbent on this hook — but it is the same programmatic-network competitor-shape that has killed ideas in other niches (home-security-device-compat, power-tool-battery-compat), worth watching as a scale-into-adjacent-niches risk.
  - **AIO re-probe (manual checkpoint, `&udm=14` control used):** tested 3 head queries, one per named digital-hybrid game.
    - `is the mansions of madness companion app required to play` — **full AI Overview**, fully answers: "Yes, the companion app is required to play Mansions of Madness Second Edition," plus what the app does, plus platform availability (iOS/Android/Mac/PC via Google Play/Apple/Steam), plus the 1st-vs-2nd-edition distinction. AIO even proactively offers "Alternative fan-made apps or conversion kits?" and "device requirements/storage size?" as follow-ups.
    - `is the xcom board game app required to play offline` — no full AIO rendered in this session, but the top web-result snippet already gives the complete answer: "XCOM board game sessions can run without an app... Play Rules Offline on Your Own."
    - `chronicles of crime app required offline mode` — same pattern: top snippet states "This game requires an app to play, but once downloaded, no Internet connection is needed to play."
    - **3/3 tested games got an instant, complete answer to "is the app required + what platforms" with no compute wedge left on that specific fact.** This is a real commoditization of the tool's "mandatory/optional + platform support" component.
    - **The wedge that survives:** `what happens if mansions of madness app is discontinued` returned **no synthesized verdict** — only scattered, dated, inconclusive community threads (Facebook 160+ comment thread genuinely worried; Reddit top answer hedging "might get phased out... perhaps"). Nobody — including AIO — has published a current-as-of-today support-status readout or a fallback recommendation. That's the idea's actual differentiator (per idea.md: "current support status... and the safest fallback"), and it survives AIO contact.
  - **Net read:** not a kill-level finding (the harder, differentiated half of the hook has no AIO answer), but the easier, more search-visible half (the literal "is it required" fact most autocomplete/SE traffic is asking) is now commodity. That's a real weakness, not a pass.

**Hook depth: strong** — the future-proofing fear is user-originated, not invented: independently evidenced on Facebook (Solo Board Gamers group, 160+ comments) and Reddit (r/MansionsOfMadness2E, explicit "will I always be able to play this game?"), both predating this idea and both unanswered by any synthesized current-status source.

**Monetization: weak** — same read as scout, confirmed via program-fact verification.
  - Amazon Associates Operating Agreement (fetched 2026-09-19, reused from this session's sibling token-capsule-shopping-planner dossier — same program, same day): tagged Special Links, 24-hour session extending to 89 days on cart-add, mandatory "As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases" disclosure — https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/agreement
  - Exact Toys & Games commission rate is login-gated (`.../help/operating/schedule` redirects to sign-in) — unverified, same gap as every other board-games dossier this batch.
  - Outputs are technically linkable (the base game itself, or a recommended non-digital-dependent alternative game — both are standard ASIN-addressable products), but **most rows have no purchasable "fix"** — a user learning their app is at risk can't buy their way out of that fact for the specific edition they own. This is the structural monetization weakness scout flagged, confirmed rather than disproven: the route survives (narrowly), it just doesn't clear to strong.
  - **Keyword Planner top-up:** the only monetization-CPC keyword requested that returned data ("xcom the board game price", 50 bucket) had **no bid data** (blank Low/High). Per the rubric, blank/low bids never downgrade an affiliate-route idea — Monetization stays weak on program-fact grounds, unchanged by this null result.

**Feasibility & sustainable upkeep: weak.**
  - Sources exist and are checkable: official app store listings (Google Play/Apple, each with "updated on" dates), publisher news pages (Fantasy Flight Games' own news feed is actively maintained — "Available Now: September 15" news post, dated 4 days before this run, directly references the Mansions of Madness app), and community reports (Reddit/BGG threads that surface problems in near-real-time).
  - But per-row verification of "is support still current" is inherently recurring and judgment-heavy — not a one-time hand-verify like a static spec. Bounding to the named list in idea.md (~10-15 games: Alchemists, XCOM, Chronicles of Crime, Detective, Space Cadets, Mansions of Madness 2e, District Noir, Sherlock Holmes Consulting Detective, etc.) keeps it plausible, but no dig this run produced a bottom-up hrs/week estimate that credibly clears the ~3hr/week cap with confidence — judged weak, matching scout's "highest maintenance-risk of the five" read, not kill-level (the sources are real and accessible).

**Data moat: weak.**
  - The barrier is real: verifying "does this game's app still work, and what's the safest fallback" is a judgment call an AI content farm can't shortcut from a single source — it requires synthesizing app-store update dates, publisher statements, and community reports per game.
  - But the long-tail surface is thin: the dataset's structure yields one page per named digital-hybrid game, and that list is short (the "digital-hybrid board game" category is inherently a small, bounded niche-within-a-niche) — the smallest surface of the batch alongside token-capsule-shopping-planner, per scout's read, which this dig did not overturn.

## Traffic sanity-check

Viability bar: ≈4,300–11,500 visitors/mo (blended ads+affiliate) for $100/mo, quoted from the ticket-03 reference table, not recomputed. **With the CSV now in hand, this reads worse than the pre-CSV opinion did.** The core head keyword tops out at the 50 bucket (Google Keyword Planner's lowest measurable tier), and 17 of 19 requested keywords — including every phrasing for both hook halves — returned no measurable volume at all. The page surface is also bounded to roughly 10-20 named digital-hybrid games (idea.md's own scoping, needed to keep upkeep bounded). A ≤50-bucket head term with an absent long-tail keyword universe and a ~10-20-page surface plausibly cannot reach the 4,300+ visitor/mo floor the $100/mo bar implies — this is a directional read (labelled opinion per the rubric), not a kill-level finding, but it's now backed by real bucket data rather than conjecture. The real demand documented above (Facebook/Reddit/SE) is genuine but appears to route through communities and forums rather than Google search — a durable-but-hard-to-monetize-via-SEO pattern.

## Risks & unknowns

- **AIO behavior generalization** — tested 3 of the ~10-15 named games; the "is it required" fact was answered instantly for all 3, suggesting this generalizes across the bounded list (each game's app-required status is well-documented on its own store listing/reviews), but not confirmed for every named game (Alchemists, Detective, Space Cadets, District Noir, Sherlock Holmes Consulting Detective not individually tested this run).
- **Exact Amazon Toys & Games commission rate** — unknown, requires logged-in Associates Central access (same gap as every board-games dossier this batch).
- **Upkeep hrs/week** — no bottom-up estimate produced this run beyond "bounded list keeps it plausible"; a real per-game touch-time estimate (checking store listing update dates + publisher news + community reports, per game, per quarter) is owed before build.
- **workswithchecker.com and similar AI-compatibility-checker networks** — don't currently cover board games, but the pattern (broad templated network expanding category by category) is a scale-in risk worth a periodic re-check, not just at this run.
