# Niche: tire-size-fitment

## Target market
US truck/SUV owners changing tire size: going up a size after a leveling kit or lift, picking between two sizes, or checking what fits their vehicle. Buying moment: right before a $800–$2,000 tire (and often wheel/kit) purchase.

## Rationale
Keyword Planner (US, Sep 2025–Aug 2026, pipeline/demand-csv/2026-10-09-demand-screen.csv) shows big head demand: "tire size calculator" 500K/mo, "tire size chart" 50K, "tire size comparison" 50K, "285 70r17 in inches" 50K, "what size tires fit my car" 5K. The head terms are served by many calculators already (tiresize.com, tirecalculatorhub.com, tiremath.com, 1010tires.com). The open question is whether a per-vehicle fit verdict (lift/level + wheel offset + trimming) is served outside Tacoma. Tire Rack pays 6% on orders that average over $400.

## Seeds
- Will size X fit my truck at my lift/level and wheel offset, and what trimming does it need?
- How tall is a given size really, by tire model (nominal vs published diameter)?
- Size-vs-size comparisons (e.g. 275/65R18 vs 275/70R18)
- OEM size by vehicle/trim plus plus-size alternatives
