# garden-planting-calendar

## Target market
US home vegetable/flower/bulb gardeners who need *their* dates: ZIP → frost dates (NOAA normals, at a chosen risk level) → when to start seeds indoors, transplant, direct-sow, plant fall bulbs/garlic/grass seed, and succession-sow. Recurring use: several times per season, every year.

## Rationale
Measured demand (Keyword Planner, US, Sep 2025–Aug 2026, `pipeline/demand-csv/2026-10-09-demand-screen.csv`): "planting zone by zip code" 50K/mo bucket; "when to plant garlic/tomatoes/grass seed/potatoes/tulip bulbs" 5K each; "planting calendar", "last frost date", "garden planner", "seed starting calculator" 5K each. Incumbents exist but each covers one slice (garden.org = frost probabilities, no crop schedule; Johnny's = crop schedule, needs a typed frost date; Almanac = ZIP calendar, ad-choked Raptive page). High-RPM Home & Garden category; Amazon Lawn & Garden 3%.

## Seeds
- planting calendar / vegetable planting calendar by zip code
- last frost date / first frost date / frost dates by zip code
- seed starting calculator
- when to plant <crop> (garlic, tomatoes, potatoes, tulip bulbs, grass seed) [in <state>/zone N]
- garden planner
