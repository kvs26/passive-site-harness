Frequently asked questions

Will any gadget fit any battery from the same brand?

Usually within a single platform (e.g. Milwaukee M18), but not across a brand's sub-platforms (M12 ≠ M18). The finder filters by exact platform to avoid this trap.

What does "needs adapter" mean?

The gadget was built for a different battery; a third-party adapter bridges yours to it. We name the specific adapter and flag the row so you don't assume a native fit.

Are adapters safe?

Many third-party adapters lack the over-discharge protection built into native tools, so we surface that caveat on every adapter row. Use them with that in mind.

Is the battery included?

Almost never — most of these gadgets ship as bare tools. Every row flags battery-included status.
