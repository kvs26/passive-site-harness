How we verify compatibility

This is the trust layer of the site — the methodology / data page. Every row in the finder is hand-checked; nothing is auto-scraped and published unseen.

What we check for every product

  • Battery interface — voltage and rail, confirmed against the manufacturer spec page.
  • Fit type — native (made by the platform owner), direct-fit (third-party that mounts the battery directly), or needs-adapter (with the specific adapter named).
  • Battery included? — flagged, because most gadgets ship bare.
  • Adapter safety caveat — adapter rows carry the community over-discharge note.

How often it updates

Monthly sweeps add newly released gadgets and re-check that buy links still resolve; dead links fall back to a search URL rather than being silently dropped.
