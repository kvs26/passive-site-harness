# Buying-intent niche stance

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 03, 05

## Question

Affiliate + AdSense ship on every site, and affiliate pays ~100× per converting visitor — so should scout structurally *prefer* purchase-adjacent niches/ideas ("is the visitor about to buy something?" as a scouting question or gate), and how hard? Decide: (a) whether buying-intent is a hard gate, a ranked preference, or a graded signal; (b) what evidence proves it at scout time (affiliate program exists for the niche, product-purchase queries in autocomplete, competitor sites carry affiliate links); (c) how the Keyword Planner checkpoint re-weights if monetization becomes primary; (d) whether pure-ads ideas are second-class-but-allowed or dead. Output: exact wording for tickets 08–09.

## Answer

**Four decisions, grilled one-by-one and confirmed:**

**1. Shape (a): hard gate + ranked preference on top ("Option B").** Monetization fit becomes a new scout **gate 7**, disjunctive per ticket 05's edge part 4 (affiliate-route OR high-RPM-route); on top of it, when more candidates pass than the 3–5 emit slots, **buying-adjacent candidates outrank pure high-RPM ones**. A buying-intent-only hard gate was rejected — it would kill the 7.5K-pv high-RPM ads path ticket 03 showed is the easiest documented route to $100/mo.

**2. Evidence at scout time (b): two cheap facts, minutes per candidate.** The gate passes on either: **(a) affiliate — strict version**: the *specific products the tool would surface* are affiliate-linkable via a **named** program ("Amazon Pets 3%", "Chewy 4%" — Amazon is the common default but any legit network counts, judged idea-by-idea); or **(b) high-RPM**: the niche maps to a high-RPM ad category per ticket 03's RPM table (Home & Garden-class, ~5×). The *ranking* scores buying-intent strength from free by-products already in hand: purchase-shaped autocomplete completions from the suggest fan-out + affiliate links spotted on incumbents during the gate-4 SERP probe. No new fetching; every claim names its program or RPM category.

**3. Keyword Planner re-weighting (c): split threshold by route, no structural change.** CSV stays one evidence piece; the no-CSV-caps-confidence-at-medium rule stays. But the flat ~$0.50 bid bar splits: **high-RPM-route ideas** need top-of-page LOW bid **≥ ~$1.00 USD-equiv** on head terms (expensive clicks are the premise); **affiliate-route ideas** treat bids as *supporting-only* — low bids never downgrade them (advertisers underbid informational queries that still convert via 24-h-cookie links); their Monetization signal grades on evidence-level re-verification of program facts instead. Planner never becomes primary — it can't see affiliate economics, and ticket 03 showed the money math is blended.

**4. Pure-ads ideas (d): second-class-but-allowed, strictly via the high-RPM side.** Low-RPM pure-ads ideas are **dead** (fail gate 7; ~43K pv/mo for $100 at $2.33 RPM with no affiliate lever — retroactively explains several graveyard niches). High-RPM pure-ads ideas pass but rank below buying-adjacent candidates and carry the stiffer $1.00 bid bar. No third path, no "huge demand" exception. **Consequence flagged and accepted:** occasion-poem-finder and poetry-craft-tools niches are structurally dead under gate 7 (no products, low RPM) — feeds the re-triage fog patch.

**Exact wording for tickets 08–09 (confirmed verbatim):**

Scout gate 7 (ticket 08):

> 7. **No monetization fit** — fails both routes: **(a) affiliate** — the specific products/items the tool would surface are not affiliate-linkable via a *named* program (name it: "Amazon Pets 3%", "Chewy 4%" — any legit network, judged idea-by-idea); and **(b) high-RPM** — the niche doesn't map to a high-RPM ad category (Home & Garden-class, ~5× baseline, per the ticket-03 RPM table). Passing either route passes the gate; record *which* route passed in the run log.

Scout emit-ranking rule (ticket 08):

> When more candidates pass the gates than emit slots, rank affiliate-route candidates above pure high-RPM candidates. Buying-intent strength = purchase-shaped autocomplete completions ("best X for Y", "X vs Y", brand+model) from the suggest fan-out already run, plus affiliate links/disclosures spotted on incumbents during the gate-4 SERP probe. No extra fetching; cite what's already in hand.

Validate signal 4 replacement (ticket 09):

> **4. Monetization** — evidence-level re-check of scout gate 7, graded by route. **Affiliate-route:** fetch the program's terms page; quote rate/cookie verbatim; confirm the tool's actual outputs are linkable products. Planner bids are supporting-only — low bids never downgrade an affiliate-route idea. **High-RPM-route:** requires the Keyword Planner CSV; top-of-page LOW bid **≥ ~$1.00 USD-equiv** on head terms = one evidence piece toward strong (currency conversion shown, raw figures verbatim). **Strong** = route facts verified at evidence level; **weak** = program exists but fit is partial (some outputs unlinkable, bids near the bar); kill-level finding = neither route survives evidence contact.

No new tickets surfaced — 08 and 09 consume this wording directly. Fog cleared: the "Keyword Planner checkpoint's role" patch in Not yet specified is answered by decision 3 above (no separate ticket needed).
