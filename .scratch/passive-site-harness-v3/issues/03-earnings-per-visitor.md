# Realistic 2026 earnings per visitor

Type: research
Status: resolved

## Question

What does one visitor actually pay in 2026, so the ~$100–300/mo bar converts into a traffic target we can sanity-check ideas against? From primary sources (ad networks' own published RPM data/floors, Amazon Associates / Chewy-Partnerize / Home Depot / CJ commission schedules, documented niche-site income reports with methodology): (a) realistic AdSense/Ezoic-class RPM ranges for small niche tool sites (not premium publishers); (b) affiliate commission rates + typical conversion rates for the pipeline's niche families (pet supplies, appliances/home, kitchen/food, crafts/printables); (c) a simple visitors-needed table: monthly visitors required to hit $100 and $300 under ads-only, affiliate-only, and blended assumptions — every input cited, `unknown` where no source exists.

## Answer

Full findings: [research/03-earnings-per-visitor.md](../research/03-earnings-per-visitor.md).

- RPM (Google's own AdSense calculator, Americas): Pets ~$2.33, Reference ~$2.33, Food & Drink ~$2.50, Hobbies ~$2.67 — but **Home & Garden ~$13.33 (5×)**. Entry bars: AdSense none; Journey by Mediavine 1,000 sessions/30d (realistic first upgrade; bans "excessive AI" content); Raptive 25K pv + 6-month-old domain; Ezoic now 250K MAU (out of reach).
- Affiliate: Amazon Pets/Home 3%, Kitchen 4.5%, 24-h cookie, 3-sales/180-days account rule, $3 pet-food bounty; Chewy 4%/15-day cookie. CTR/CVR: no citable source — carried as explicit unknowns.
- Visitors needed: ads-only $100/mo ≈ 43K pv (pets) or **7.5K pv (home & garden)**; $300/mo ≈ 129K / 22.5K. Blended (assumptions labeled): $100/mo ≈ 4.3–11.5K visitors; $300/mo ≈ 13–34.5K.
- Reality check: only 1.74% of new pages reach top-10 within a year (Ahrefs); the one documented case ramped $0→$2.4K/mo over ~15 months with ~300 articles and $20K spent. **Verdict: $100/mo in 6–9 months plausible mainly in high-RPM (home/appliance) niches with blended monetization; $300/mo in 6–9 months unsupported by any cited evidence.** Feeds ticket 05 directly — the viability bar itself may need re-setting.
