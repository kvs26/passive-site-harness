# Graveyard postmortem — why 20 of 26 pipeline ideas died, and what scout could have caught

Repo-internal analysis, 2026-08-31. Every claim cites the specific `idea.md` / `dossier.md` (paths relative to repo root). No invented numbers.

## 0. Actual counts (from [pipeline/board.md](../../pipeline/board.md))

| Status | Count | Ideas |
|---|---|---|
| validated | 2 | pet-food-tools: dog-treat-portion-calculator, can-my-dog-eat-database |

Of the 20 rejections, **18 were killed on gate 4 / hook-already-served** (the two exceptions: washer-capacity-converter was primarily gate 1 — Google AI Overview; sticker-pack-preflight died on *no demand for the differentiator*, its incumbent having been a scout misread). 18 of the 20 rejected candidates were emitted **already flagged "weak" or gate-4/5 near-miss** in their own Gate results — the "weak candidates are kept" rule in [.agents/skills/idea-scout/SKILL.md](../../.agents/skills/idea-scout/SKILL.md) forwarded nearly every eventual corpse to validate.

## 1. What scout currently checks (and the gap)

From [.agents/skills/idea-scout/SKILL.md](../../.agents/skills/idea-scout/SKILL.md):

- **Pass 3** — "DDG SERPs for candidate queries; competitor sitemap/robots inspection." It runs *demand-phrased* queries (the autocomplete phrasings from Pass 1), not *hook-phrased / tool-intent* queries, and nothing forces scout to **fetch and open** the incumbents it sees on the SERP.
- **Gate 4** — "can't state in one sentence why it beats the incumbent SERP results." This tests whether a hook is *articulable*, not whether the hook is *verifiably absent from the market*. A candidate passes gate 4 by writing a good sentence.
- The escape hatch is used constantly: idea files say "Validate MUST open emoji.gg/validate" ([sticker-pack-preflight/idea.md](../../pipeline/sticker-pack-tools/ideas/sticker-pack-preflight/idea.md)), "itemfits.com must be inspected at validate" ([appliance-fit-checker/idea.md](../../pipeline/home-appliance-lookup/ideas/appliance-fit-checker/idea.md)), "Differentiator … not confirmed absent — validate must open these" ([indian-menu-rotation-builder/idea.md](../../pipeline/indian-home-meal-planner/ideas/indian-menu-rotation-builder/idea.md)), "Existing competitor calculators: not observed in the SERPs fetched this run — presence unknown" ([chocolate-toxicity-calculator/idea.md](../../pipeline/pet-food-tools/ideas/chocolate-toxicity-calculator/idea.md)).

## 3. The two survivors vs the dead

**can-my-dog-eat-database** ([idea.md](../../pipeline/pet-food-tools/ideas/can-my-dog-eat-database/idea.md)) and **dog-treat-portion-calculator** ([idea.md](../../pipeline/pet-food-tools/ideas/dog-treat-portion-calculator/idea.md)) share five properties the dead lack:

1. **Scout verified the gap against the strongest incumbent at scout time.** dog-treat's idea.md: "Strongest incumbent inspected (caneat.pet/tools, fetched this run) … Confirmed gap"; and its SERP check was run on the *exact tool-intent query* ("how many blueberries can a dog eat per day calculator" → "every result a static article; **no calculator despite the query**"). can-my-dog-eat's validate confirmed the hook "absent from the two interactive competitors found" (idea.md History). Contrast: 5+ dead ideas were emitted with an explicitly *unopened* named incumbent (§2 items 4, 6, 7, 11, 14) and one with "presence unknown" (item 20).
2. **N-row dataset + per-item long-tail pages, not a single artifact.** Both are ~100–200+ food-item databases over a stable public source (USDA FoodData Central), generating thousands of per-food/per-weight long-tail targets. The dead are overwhelmingly single-artifact tools or reference pages (one converter, one matrix, one directory page) where **one incumbent page serves the entire hook** — which is exactly how they died (writoryofficial one article, KakaMeme one article, khanakyabanau one tool, itemfits one tool).
3. **Per-query computation an article can't do.** Both compute per-(food × dog-weight) outputs (toxic-dose banding; 10%-kcal treat budget). Dead "computation" ideas either had no real formula (washer cu ft↔kg — [dossier](../../pipeline/home-appliance-lookup/ideas/washer-capacity-converter/dossier.md): "no true conversion formula") or computed something incumbents already compute (chocolate, cat, dog-food calculators).
4. **Keyword-Planner-confirmed panic/recurrence head demand.** "can dogs eat grapes" 500K bucket, toxicity-calculator 50K +900% YoY; dog-treat per-food group ~13K bucket (idea.md Histories). No dead idea ever reached a CSV — every one died first on hook.
5. **Survivable weak spots were *not* gate 4.** Both survivors' emission near-misses were gate 1 (answer-it), and dog-treat's unique-data grade stayed weak (FurCalc precedent) without killing it. In the graveyard, the emission near-miss was gate 4 (hook) in the large majority of cases — **a gate-4 near-miss at emission was a near-perfect death predictor** (poems-by-form, fixed-forms, preflight, monetization, fridge-filter, appliance-fit, compat-matrix, ai-employee, non-dev, guinea-pig, menu-rotation all emitted as gate-4 near-miss/weak and all died on gate 4).

SERP state at scout time is the cleanest discriminator: survivors' hook-intent SERPs showed *static articles despite tool-shaped queries*; the dead's hook-intent SERPs (when finally run at validate) showed dedicated tools/pages.

## 4. Ranked scout-time changes (each grounded in named kills)

1. **Hook-intent SERP probe (upgrade Pass 3 + gate 4): before emitting, run 2–3 queries phrased as the *hook/tool/directory intent* (not the demand phrasing) and fail gate 4 if a dedicated incumbent ranks.** Would have caught: chocolate-toxicity ("my dog ate chocolate calculator"), dog-food-calculator ("dog food calculator by weight and age"), cat-portion ("cat food portion calculator"), xylitol ("list of products with xylitol toxic to dogs"), poems-by-form ("poems in iambic pentameter"), scansion ("scansion practice with answers"), fixed-forms ("poetic forms with examples and templates"), read-aloud ("how to read a poem at a funeral" + "reading time calculator"), sticker-monetization ("sell line stickers"), paths-reference ("claude code skills location" — scout's own autocomplete phrase), non-dev ("claude skills for marketing"), compat-matrix ("agent skills compatibility matrix"), ai-employee ("claude skills bundle"), fridge-filter ("refrigerator water filter compatibility finder", marginal). **≈ 13–14 of 20 kills prevented.** Every one of these queries is documented as the actual validate kill query in the respective dossier.
2. **"Open the named incumbent" rule: an idea may not be emitted while its own Gate-4/Evidence text names an unfetched incumbent URL or defers inspection to validate.** Directly grounded in: appliance-fit (itemfits "must be inspected at validate"), indian-menu-rotation (khanakyabanau "validate must open these"), reprint-clear (funeralverses seen on SERP, never opened), sticker-pack-preflight (emoji.gg misread from SERP title alone), ai-employee (analoghq found, not dug), guinea-pig (both incumbents already fetched — needs the companion threshold below), chocolate ("presence unknown" emission). **≈ 6–7 kills prevented, ~4 unique beyond change 1.**
3. **Shape filter (new gate): prefer N-row dataset + per-item pages + per-query computation; single-artifact tools/reference pages require an *exceptional* hook because one incumbent page can serve them entirely.** Grounded in the survivor contrast (§3.2): the single-artifact dead include washer-converter, appliance-fit, menu-rotation, preflight, monetization-directory, paths-reference, compat-matrix, fixed-forms, read-aloud, reprint-clear (~10 kills fit the shape). As a hard gate it's blunt; as a scoring penalty it would have demoted most of the graveyard below emission threshold. **Prevents ~8–10 kills in combination with 1–2, few uniquely.**
4. **AI Overview probe (upgrade gate 1): fetch the head query on Google (not DDG — DDG has no AIO) and kill/demote if AIO fully answers the hook.** Grounded in: washer-capacity (the only pure gate-1 kill — [dossier](../../pipeline/home-appliance-lookup/ideas/washer-capacity-converter/dossier.md)) and parked roti-sabzi (AIO answers head pairing query). **1 kill + 1 park prevented — uniquely catches what change 1 misses**, since DDG-based Pass 3 is structurally blind to AIO.
5. **Differentiator-demand fan-out: run the differentiator phrasing itself through fanout.py before emitting; if nobody searches for the differentiator, the "unserved cut" is worthless.** Grounded in: sticker-pack-preflight ("fan-out on validate/check/batch queries returned nothing on-topic" — [dossier](../../pipeline/sticker-pack-tools/ideas/sticker-pack-preflight/dossier.md)) and sticker-monetization ("Direct US search-volume for 'sell sticker packs': unknown (not measured this run)" — its own idea.md). **≈ 2 kills, 1 unique** (preflight is the only kill changes 1–2 don't fully cover, since its incumbent genuinely didn't exist).
6. **Tighten the "weak candidates are kept" rule: a gate-4 near-miss may be kept, but ≥2 credible incumbents already serving an adjacent shape ⇒ kill at scout.** Grounded in guinea-pig (scout had fetched guineapigtube *and* NibbleCheck and still emitted) and the §3.5 pattern (gate-4-near-miss emission ≈ death). This converts validate's most common outcome into a scout-time run-log kill, saving a full validate session per idea.

## 5. Kills no scout change could have prevented — the freshness race

Strictly documented in-window market movement (scout → validate) exists for **one** idea: **agent-skills-compat-matrix**, where the porex-bot incumbent went from 0-star README (scout, 08-25) to a full static site with scheduled crawler (validate, 08-29), and aipm-registry re-stamped "last checked 28 Aug 2026" — 3 days post-scout ([dossier](../../pipeline/agent-skills-hub/ideas/agent-skills-compat-matrix/dossier.md)). Even there, vibekk (2026-07-22) and openagentskill existed pre-scout, so a hook-intent probe still kills it. **Count of kills that were strictly unpreventable: 0–1.**

But the sharper finding is the **shelf-life problem**: ~8 of 20 kills involved purpose-built incumbents created or updated within ~8 weeks of the scout run — filterfitnow (updated 2026-08-24, 5 days pre-scout), inspire-company (Jul 2026), filtersearcher (2026-07-14), meltflexai (2026-07-12), poemsteric (2026-07-29), storgy (© 2026), KakaMeme (2026-07-05), vibekk (2026-07-22), caninebible calculator (2026-07-08), crittercalcs/freecalculatorshub (late Jul 2026), thepetcalculator/myycat (updated 2026-08-14/16, inside the pet scout→validate window). Implication: **a clean scout-time SERP is a decaying asset.** Even with every fix in §4, the pipeline must (a) shrink scout→validate→build latency to days, (b) re-probe the hook-intent SERP immediately before build, and (c) prefer the survivor shape (§3) — deep datasets with long-tail surface area — because that shape degrades gracefully when a fresh single-page incumbent appears, whereas single-artifact hooks die from one competitor's July blog post.
