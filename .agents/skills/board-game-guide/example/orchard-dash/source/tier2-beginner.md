# Beginner tactics

> **Assumes:** you know the goal and the turn order from [Tier 1](#tier1-how-to-play).

## First five turns

### Take the dashed trees early

**Why it works.** Dashed trees give one bonus apple, and the path is short. A bonus early means more apples over the whole game.

**When it applies.** Whenever a dashed tree is within one die roll of your pawn.

**When it fails.** If the dashed tree is far away and a plain tree gives the same apples, the safe move is as good.

**Example.** You are on tree 1 and roll a 3. Tree 4 is dashed. Move there and take the bonus.

**Common mistake.** Stopping on a plain tree "to be safe". Fix: count the apples first, then decide.

## Reading the board

### Count apples, not steps

**Why it works.** The goal is apples. Distance only matters when it changes apples.

**When it applies.** Any turn where you have a choice.

**When it fails.** At the very end, when the last step lets you finish the game.

**Example.** A move of 2 trees that gives 3 apples beats a move of 4 trees that gives 1.

**Common mistake.** Chasing the furthest tree. Fix: write down apples per move for each option.
