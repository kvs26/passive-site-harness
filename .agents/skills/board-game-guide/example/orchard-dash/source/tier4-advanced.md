# Advanced tactics

> **Assumes:** you plan whole games and know the [glossary](#quick-reference--glossary).

## Reading opponents

### Block the tree your rival needs

**Why it works.** A rival who is one apple short often needs one exact tree. Standing there first removes it.

**When it applies.** The leader is within 2 apples of the goal.

**When it fails.** If blocking costs you more apples than it costs them, you fall behind.

**Example.** A rival has 8 apples and sits two trees before a 2-apple tree. You can reach it first. Take it.

**Common mistake.** Blocking too early. Fix: wait until the leader is within 2 apples.
