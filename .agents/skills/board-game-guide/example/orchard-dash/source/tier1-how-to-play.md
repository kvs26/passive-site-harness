# How to play

> **Assumes:** nothing. You have never played. Words in *italics* are in the [glossary](#quick-reference--glossary).

## Goal and setup

Be the first player to collect **10 apples**. Each player gets a basket and a pawn. Put all pawns on the start tree.

## Your turn

1. Roll the die.
2. Move your pawn that many trees forward.
3. Take the apples shown on that tree.

## Worked example

Mia is on tree 2. She rolls a 3, so she moves to tree 5. Tree 5 shows 2 apples, so she takes 2. She now has 4 apples. It is the next player's turn.
