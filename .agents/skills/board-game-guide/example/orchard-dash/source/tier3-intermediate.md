# Intermediate tactics

> **Assumes:** you have played a few games and use the [beginner tactics](#tier2-beginner).

## Planning the whole game

### Aim for the lucky total

**Why it works.** With two dice, a total of 7 comes up 6 times out of 36 (one in six). A total of 2 comes up once in 36. Standing where a likely total lands you on a good tree wins more often.

**When it applies.** In the variant where you roll two dice.

**When it fails.** If a rival will take the tree first, the lucky total gives you nothing.

**Example.** You are on tree 1 and tree 8 gives 3 apples. A 7 is the most likely roll, so tree 8 is a good target.

**Common mistake.** Treating every total as equal. Fix: remember that middle totals are the most common.
